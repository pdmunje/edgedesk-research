# ES rebound after recent weakness — corrected recheck

Question: did price below a falling 120–180-bar simple average precede stronger upward changes 240 observed eligible bars later? Finding: the three original rules had modestly higher positive-close fractions, tiny extra mean per signal minute, and larger positive equal-day differences in the already examined January 2025–September 2026 period. These weighting methods answer different questions; none is an executed trading result.

## Rules and timing

SMA(N,t)=sum(close[t-N+1] through close[t])/N. Original fixed 240-bar upward-outcome rules: N,K=(120,60),(120,30),(180,60); close[t]<SMA(N,t) AND SMA(N,t)<SMA(N,t-K). Both comparisons strict; missing values do not qualify. Earlier rejected 60-bar diagnostic: MOM480_negative, close[t]<close[t-480]. Keep these definitions unchanged; frozen-definitions.json also records original top diagnostics and rankings as historical metadata, not recomputed current metrics.

Remove New York 17:00–18:00 maintenance rows before every feature and future window. Preserve roll segment identity from the original rows first. Reset features at rolls; no forward window across roll or the four broad evaluation periods. A trading date starts at 18:00 local on the previous calendar date, DST aware. Windows and horizons count observed eligible bars, not elapsed clock minutes; gaps/weekends/sessions can be crossed without filling missing bars. Final session and year are partial. The data audit retains source quality/source counts before removal and observed gap counts after removal.

Return=(close[t+H]-close[t])/close[t]. Percentage=100×return; basis points=10,000×return, so 1 basis point=0.01%. At an invented price of 6,000, 1 basis point=0.60 point. Close is a measurement reference known only at completion, with no actual fill claim. Positive means strictly >0; flat results stay in the denominator. Favourable=max(0,max(high[t+1:t+H])-close[t]); adverse=max(0,close[t]-min(low[t+1:t+H])). Tick=0.25 point. Target=+32 ticks=+8 points, stop=−25 ticks=−6.25 points; scan future bars in order, with a first bar touching both classified unknown. No touch is a separate category. Neither is silently counted as a target win.

## Reference, weighting and uncertainty

Separate each period/horizon. Reference at a session-minute slot=mean of all eligible later returns at that slot, including qualifying observations themselves and dates later than the anchor. Retrospective, no matched volatility/year or live forecast claim. Extra return=return-reference. Equal-minute extra mean=mean of every signal-minute extra return. Equal-day extra mean=mean of daily averages among dates with a signal. Invented example: three +1bp signals on day A and one −2bp signal on day B produce +0.25bp per minute but −0.50bp per day. The difference comes from weighting.

For uncertainty, resample these signal-day means with replacement 300 times; take the 2.5%/97.5% quantiles of the resampled average. Deterministic seed per candidate/period/horizon = first eight bytes of SHA-256 of `20261002|candidate|period|horizon`, interpreted little-endian. Use NumPy default_rng. This is reproducibility, not a choice fitted to results. Does not remove cross-day dependence, adjust all model searches or refit reference means per resample. Intervals concern the equal-day extra mean, not per-minute effects or 95% of future trades.

## Full search and earlier selection

Fifteen windows: [3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, 360, 480]. Each has 2 momentum signs and 3 average types × (2 price sides + 4 slope horizons × 2 aligned signs)=32 states. Total 480 states, 1920 development rows across 2 horizons and 2 periods. Momentum=(current−N-bars-earlier close)/current close; distance=(current close−average)/current close. Weighted average weights 1..N, latest N; exponential alpha 2/(N+1), seed first close and expose after N bars. All reset at rolls. Five-group edges: linear 20/40/60/80 percentiles of available discovery feature values, reused in validation, upper group on equality. 240 aggregate five-group rows. Bucket-index correlation is Pearson correlation of group number and group extra mean. Clear: correlation≥.9 and at least75% adjacent increases; approximate≥.6; weak≥.25; otherwise non-monotonic. This is descriptive ordering, not a significance test.

Periods: 2010–2014 older check; 2015–2022 development; 2023–2024 comparison; 2025 through available September 2026 later period. The last was already examined before this recheck. Source research gates: excess positive fraction≥.25 percentage point AND excess mean≥.10bp in BOTH development/comparison periods, comparison day mean>0 and coverage≥5%; at least 2 other same-family/type/slope windows with positive extra minute mean in both periods and length between half/twice the candidate. Score=min(two minute means)+.1×min(two probability differences). Source procedure retains top 3 passes/top 5 diagnostics. selection-audit.json repeats this repaired development-only procedure; it does not replace original fixed rules or claim a fresh test. The entire grid remains in window_scan.csv, including failures. Source threshold 0.1bp is only 0.06 point at illustrative 6000, below a tick; it is not an economic viability hurdle.

## Aggregate column guide

candidate/family/average_type/window/slope_horizon identify a definition; period and forward_horizon identify evaluation dates and observed-bar count. observations=eligible qualifying decisions; trading_days=distinct New York signal dates; years=distinct decision trading years; coverage_pct=observations/all eligible horizon outcomes within that period×100. positive_pct=100×positive outcomes/observations including flat outcomes; baseline_positive_pct=mean slot-positive fraction×100; excess_positive_pp=difference in percentage points. mean_return_pct and baseline_mean_return_pct are percentage changes. excess_mean_bp is the equal-minute extra return×10,000. day_balanced_excess_bp is the equal-signal-day extra mean×10,000; day_block_ci_low_bp/high_bp are that average's resampling bounds. mean_MFE_points/mean_MAE_points are favourable/adverse mean magnitudes; MFE_MAE_ratio is their ratio of means. target_first_pct/stop_first_pct/no_touch_pct/ambiguous_first_touch_pct partition the same observations. median_return_pct and p10/p25/p75/p90_return_pct are close-change distribution statistics in percent. Annual rows use anchor trading year and a same-year clock reference; windows can finish next year inside one broad evaluation period. Five-group tables report each group's extra mean in bp and extra positive fraction in pp; quintile group counts are not independent experiments. Full grid functions checked; not every cell independently recounted.

## Corrections, verification and interpretation

Original code let maintenance rows enter features/outcomes and allowed future windows across evaluation-period boundaries; those are corrected. Nonnegative excursions and separate double-touch unknowns/no-touch categories are corrected too. Earlier-result-comparison.csv records old versus repaired later-period summary values; do not treat them as newly identical. Existing frozen rules retained, no later-period reselection. The older slow-regime and momentum studies share history; they are not independent confirmations. Independent fixed and annual recount: 84 rows, 2100 metric comparisons; 11 mathematical examples. Recounts use pandas rolling means, independently grouped references/day means and reversed rolling extrema. Direct future-bar matrices check barrier order, separate from the production deque/active-path method. Source fingerprint unchanged. Tolerance3e-8 relative/2e-7 absolute accommodates aggregate rounding. No actual trading costs, fills, sizing or one-position execution rule; all figures are chart measurements.
